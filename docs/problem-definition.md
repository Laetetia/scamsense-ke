# ScamSense KE: Problem Definition

## 1. Background

The increasing use of digital communication and online financial services has created opportunities for cybercriminals to exploit unsuspecting internet users.

In Kenya, individuals increasingly encounter suspicious digital communications through SMS messages, social media platforms, online advertisements and messaging applications.

These communications may involve fraudulent M-PESA notifications, fake employment opportunities, phishing links, investment schemes and impersonation of legitimate organisations.

Many users lack the technical knowledge or resources required to identify these threats before responding to them.

## 2. Problem Statement

There is a need for an accessible and understandable solution that helps individuals identify potentially fraudulent digital communications.

Existing approaches often require users to independently recognise warning signs, verify unfamiliar links or understand sophisticated social engineering techniques.

Consequently, users may unknowingly disclose sensitive information, send money to fraudsters or interact with malicious websites.

ScamSense KE proposes an AI-assisted platform that analyses suspicious messages, URLs and advertisements, identifies potential fraud indicators and provides understandable risk explanations.

## 3. Target Users

The proposed platform will primarily target:

- Mobile money users.
- Job seekers.
- Online shoppers.
- Small business owners.
- Internet users with limited cybersecurity awareness.
- Individuals who frequently receive unsolicited digital communications.

## 4. Proposed Solution

ScamSense KE will provide a platform where users can submit suspicious digital content for automated analysis.

The system will examine submitted content using rule-based detection techniques and, in later development stages, machine learning.

It will identify suspicious indicators, generate risk assessments and explain the factors contributing to the assessment.

## 5. Expected Outcomes

The project aims to:

1. Improve awareness of common digital fraud techniques.
2. Help users recognise suspicious communication patterns.
3. Provide preliminary analysis of suspicious URLs.
4. Identify potential impersonation and financial manipulation attempts.
5. Present understandable explanations of detected risks.
6. Explore machine learning approaches for identifying locally relevant scam patterns.

## 6. Project Limitations

The platform will not guarantee the detection of every fraudulent communication.

Its effectiveness will depend on the quality of detection rules, available datasets and the sophistication of the fraudulent techniques encountered.

Risk assessments will therefore be presented as guidance rather than definitive proof of fraud.

## 7. Project Vision

To develop an accessible, locally relevant cybersecurity platform that empowers Kenyan internet users to make safer decisions when interacting with digital communications.
